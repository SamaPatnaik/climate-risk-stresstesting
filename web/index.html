Method in brief

  • Hazard. Share of each division's land burned per year (BC Wildfire Service historical perimeters, 1990–2025), scaled so twice the worst division-year on record = 1.
  • Model. H = min(1, h × m); PD = PD0(1 + βPDH); LGD = LGD0 + βLGDH (uninsured loans); EL = PD × LGD × EAD. PD0 is calibrated so BC PD equals observed 90+ day arrears (0.26%) under historical climate.
  • Simulation. Each run draws one historical fire season, both betas and a default for every loan; the same draws are shared across scenarios. Portfolio tail measures come from per-run totals, never sums of division tails.
  • Framing. Scenario names follow NGFS categories and the exercise is framed against OSFI Guideline B-15; neither is claimed to be reproduced.
Data: BC Wildfire Service (Open Government Licence – BC); Statistics Canada 2021 Census and boundary files; CBA; Bank of Canada; OSFI.
